2. TCP Handshake Theory & Flag Mechanics

TCP Connection Management

Instructor: Erika Pratiwi (09:42 / 12:15)

The Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) uses a three-way handshake to establish a reliable connection. This process ensures both client and server are synchronized and ready to exchange IP packets.

Step 1: Client sends SYN (Synchronize sequence number)
Step 2: Server responds with SYN-ACK (Acknowledge and Syn sequence number)
Step 3: Client responds with ACK (Acknowledge sequence)
